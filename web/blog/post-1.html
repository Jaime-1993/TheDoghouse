Tips for Traveling with Your Dog

Published on August 22, 2026

Traveling with your dog can be a wonderful experience, but it requires careful planning and preparation. Whether you're going on a road trip or flying to a new destination, ensuring your dog's safety and comfort is essential. Here are some tips to make your journey smooth and enjoyable.

Preparation Before You Go

Start preparing well in advance:

  • Visit the vet – Get a health check-up and ensure vaccinations are up to date
  • Pack a travel kit – Food, water, bowls, leash, waste bags, medication, and a first-aid kit
  • Update ID tags – Make sure your dog's collar has current contact information
  • Microchip – Consider microchipping if you haven't already

Documentation

Depending on your destination, you may need:

  • Health certificate from your vet (often required for flights and international travel)
  • Vaccination records
  • Pet passport (for European travel)
  • Import permits for certain countries

Finding Pet-Friendly Hotels

Not all hotels welcome dogs, so it's important to do your research. Use resources like The Doghouse interactive map to find pet-friendly accommodations. Look for hotels that offer:

  • Pet beds and bowls
  • Designated outdoor areas
  • Nearby parks or walking trails
  • Pet-sitting or walking services

During the Journey

While traveling, keep your dog comfortable and safe:

  • Use a secure crate or harness in the car
  • Take regular breaks for exercise and bathroom
  • Never leave your dog alone in a parked car
  • Maintain a regular feeding and walking schedule as much as possible

With proper preparation, traveling with your dog can be a rewarding experience for both of you. Use our Pet-Friendly Hotels Map to find the perfect place to stay.
