# Gantt Chart – The Doghouse

This schedule is the **proposal calendar** for the first usable version of **The Doghouse**. It matches the [interview annex](INTERVIEW_ANNEX.md) (two-to-three-month horizon, quality over a harmful rigid date), the [Scope](01_SCOPE.md), the [work breakdown](04_WBS.md), and the proposal report.

## Premises

- **Product:** real product for dog owners (site, members’ area, calculator, workshops, hotels, selling).
- **Horizon:** about **two to three months** of focused work for a usable first version.
- **Calendar role:** the milestone table below **is** the proposed plan as of **2026-10-14**. The figure is illustrative; if it disagrees with the table, the **table wins** until the SVG is redrawn. Weeks may shift during execution if quality requires; product scope does not change without a new freeze.
- **Launch workshops:** **2 free** + **4 paid**, organised by breed.
- **Invoice / receipt:** in for v1, trial/test mode first if safer.
- **Seamless site → members’ area:** signed-in members only.
- **Costs:** low at the start; no expensive custom domain required.
- **Brand:** The Doghouse only in public materials.
